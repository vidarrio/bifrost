Bifrost

The bridge between worlds — a home for Archipelago multiworlds.

Archipelago is a multiworld randomizer. Everyone plays a game of their own, but the items are shuffled across all of them, so the sword you need may be in someone else's game, and they'll find it for you. Bifrost runs those games as seasons, from signups to the last check.

  1. Sign upPick a game and upload its YAML. It's checked on the spot, so a bad option shows up now, not on generation day.
  2. GenerateWhen signups close, the season's organisers generate the multiworld, and Bifrost hosts its game server.
  3. PlayEach live season's How to join tab has the address, the Archipelago version and links to your games.
  4. Follow alongThe live board shows everyone's progress and hints, and your game's page shows what's in logic.

Track your run

  • Universal Tracker — drop-in tracker showing which of your locations are in logic right now. Works with almost any game.
  • PopTracker — visual map/item auto-tracker; grab your game's pack.
Several games also ship their own tracker (e.g. the BG3 and Pokémon packs, the PoE web tracker). Point it at your season's address, under How to join on its live board, with your slot name.
